# Game Overview

Sekigahara: What-If Battle Simulator recreates the Battle of Sekigahara under a variety of conditions and lets the player observe autonomous unit behavior and changing battlefield momentum.

[日本語版](../../game/overview.md)

## Core Design

Battle results are influenced by more than troop count.

- Unit attack and defense
- Unit morale
- Morale of individual soldier dots
- Commander leadership
- Fatigue
- Encirclement and positioning
- Overall battle conditions
- Formation
- Unit separation and defeat in detail

The game includes historically inspired scenarios as well as alternate-history scenarios with different participation conditions, troop strengths, and commander abilities.

## Visual Presentation

Version 2.0.0 introduced a more faithful terrain layout for Sekigahara and animated soldiers in zoom mode.

- Ctrl + left click: zoom mode
- Ctrl + mouse wheel: change map magnification from 1× to 50×
