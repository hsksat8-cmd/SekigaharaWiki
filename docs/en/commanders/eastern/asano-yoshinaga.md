## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with notably high loyalty (80) and command confidence (74). Starting morale is 99, while hesitation is 28.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

The son of Asano Nagamasa, Yoshinaga grew up close to the inner workings of the Toyotomi administration. His decision to support the Eastern Army at Sekigahara would affect not only his own military reputation but also the future of the Asano house.

## Character and Reputation

As a young daimyo he could aspire to honor in battle while also having to avoid reckless losses among his retainers. The tension between courage and responsibility is central to understanding his position.

## The Person and Their Background

Because his father Nagamasa held a prominent place in Toyotomi government, Yoshinaga grew up where military decisions also had political consequences. A young lord could win honor through battle, yet a single mistake might determine the lives of his relatives and retainers. It would be natural for the desire to act boldly to coexist with the need to think carefully about the household's future.

Supporting the Tokugawa side despite his family's closeness to the Toyotomi raised difficult questions about how obligations should be honored. His decision needs to be viewed not only through personal feeling but through the practical responsibilities of a daimyo entrusted with his family's survival.
