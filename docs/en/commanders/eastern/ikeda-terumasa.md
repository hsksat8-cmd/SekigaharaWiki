## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with notably high loyalty (82) and command confidence (76). Starting morale is 98, while hesitation is 32.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

The son of Ikeda Tsuneoki, Terumasa gained experience under the Oda and Toyotomi regimes. His participation in the Eastern Army and his subsequent work on Himeji Castle illustrate a career linking military victory with the establishment of stable rule.

## Character and Reputation

He can be understood as a leader who valued disciplined formations and a reliable chain of command rather than moving troops through enthusiasm alone.

## The Person and Their Background

Terumasa lost his father Tsuneoki in the Battle of Komaki and Nagakute and experienced the transition between the Oda and Toyotomi regimes firsthand. Inheriting a warrior family's name meant not merely matching his father's battlefield exploits but providing stable leadership for his relatives and retainers. With experience, the importance of discipline and preparation to the peace that followed victory may have become clearer.

After Sekigahara he received extensive lands and undertook major work at Himeji Castle. His career suggests an effort to transform success in battle into an enduring order. The vigor needed to win territory and the caution needed to govern it were not separate concerns.
