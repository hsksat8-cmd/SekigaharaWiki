## In-Game Characteristics

This is a small unit, with particularly high attack (96) and loyalty (96). Starting morale is 111, while hesitation is 8.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

One of Tokugawa Ieyasu's most celebrated generals. He fought in many of Ieyasu's campaigns and served as a senior commander at Sekigahara.

## Character and Reputation

Celebrated as an almost invincible warrior, he also represented steady command and deep loyalty to Ieyasu.
