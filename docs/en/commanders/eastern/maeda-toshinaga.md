## In-Game Characteristics

This is a large unit, with particularly high loyalty (84) and leadership (84). Starting morale is 102, while hesitation is 34.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

The eldest son of Maeda Toshiie and successor to the great Maeda domain. During the Sekigahara campaign he supported the Eastern side and fought Western-aligned forces in Hokuriku.

## Character and Reputation

A cautious political leader who worked to preserve the enormous Maeda domain under Tokugawa rule.
