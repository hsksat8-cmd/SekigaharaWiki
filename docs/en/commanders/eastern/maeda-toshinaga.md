## In-Game Characteristics

This is a large unit, with notably high loyalty (84) and leadership (84). Starting morale is 102, while hesitation is 34.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

Toshinaga inherited the extensive Maeda domain built by his father Toshiie. Its wealth also made the family an object of Tokugawa suspicion. Protecting that inheritance became a central duty, and he faced major political decisions away from the main field of Sekigahara.

## Character and Reputation

The greater a lord's power, the greater the danger of using it without restraint. His caution can be understood as the pressure felt by a family head who placed household stability above personal glory.

## The Person and Their Background

The strength Toshinaga inherited rested on productive territories and the personal relationships his father Toshiie had cultivated. Yet such strength also invited Tokugawa suspicion. No longer able to rely on his father's standing, Toshinaga had to safeguard the house through his own judgments. Simply demonstrating power was not enough.

In the tensions surrounding Sekigahara, keeping relatives and retainers out of a destructive war could matter more than proving martial courage. Caution is not necessarily cowardice. Recognizing when to concede in order to protect a great domain may itself have required resolve.
