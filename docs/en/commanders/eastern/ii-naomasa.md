## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with notably high aggressiveness (94) and loyalty (92). Starting morale is 105, while hesitation is 8.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

Raised to prominence by Tokugawa Ieyasu, Naomasa commanded the elite Red Devils and carried the expectations of a senior Tokugawa retainer. Stories of competition for the vanguard at Sekigahara are associated with him, and he fought near the front closely enough to be wounded.

## Character and Reputation

His drive for distinction was accompanied by a commander's concern for cohesion and the honor of his banner. Boldness and severity were also expressions of his responsibility to answer the trust Ieyasu had placed in him.

## The Person and Their Background

As the young head of the Ii family, Naomasa had reason to prove through his own achievements that his house deserved its restored standing. Command of the celebrated red-armored troops was prestigious, but it also imposed the burden of living up to their reputation. His reported strictness, sometimes even harshness, can be connected with the demands of keeping an elite force united.

He fought at the front at Sekigahara and sustained a wound. Even if the desire for military glory played a part, his actions also suggest a determination to repay his lord's trust and lead his men by example. Courage and exacting discipline reinforced each other in his image as a commander.
