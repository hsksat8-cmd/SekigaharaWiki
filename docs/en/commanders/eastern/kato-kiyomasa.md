## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with notably high attack (92) and aggressiveness (92). Starting morale is 107, while hesitation is 8.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

A veteran of Hideyoshi's campaigns, Kiyomasa gained renown as a formidable warrior while also devoting attention to fortifications and flood control. He did not fight in the main battle of Sekigahara, acting in Kyushu on the Eastern side.

## Character and Reputation

His aggressive style was accompanied by concern for the practical foundations of battle, from stone walls to provisions. His actions suggest a commander who understood that bold attacks depended on careful preparation.

## The Person and Their Background

Kiyomasa acquired extensive battlefield experience under Hideyoshi while also working on castles and flood control in Higo. Despite his reputation as an attacking warrior, moving a large army required supplies and an understanding of terrain. Boldness in action and meticulous preparation were not contradictory. Experience of actual warfare may have strengthened his awareness of the planning that made courage effective.

His sense of obligation to the Toyotomi and his cooperation with the Tokugawa make his political position difficult to explain in simple terms. While seeking stability for his own lands, he may also have considered the future of the house that had advanced him. His responsibilities as a territorial ruler deserve attention alongside his military reputation.
