This is a medium-sized unit, with notably high leadership (84) and command confidence (84). Starting morale is 102, while hesitation is 30.

## Brief Career

Having supported the Ryuzoji house before establishing effective authority in Hizen, Naoshige understood the complicated loyalties of Sengoku politics. During the Sekigahara period he had to consider relations with both the Toyotomi and Tokugawa in order to protect the Nabeshima family.

## Character and Reputation

He appears to have favored keeping his domain and retainers intact over the pursuit of immediate victory. Years spent surviving warfare may have reinforced his deliberate, practical judgment.

## The Person and Their Background

Naoshige consolidated influence in Hizen while serving the Ryuzoji, navigating relations in which formal rank did not always match actual power. In such circumstances, holding retainers together and preserving territory demanded sustained political skill more than visible military glory. His experience may have taught him to judge carefully when to support another leader and when to act on his own initiative.

During the Sekigahara crisis, deeper involvement in the central struggle risked destabilizing the Nabeshima lands in Kyushu. Understanding his decisions requires attention to the problem of reconciling outward loyalty with the long-term survival of his household.
