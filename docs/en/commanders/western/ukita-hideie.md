## In-Game Characteristics

This is a large unit, with particularly high loyalty (86) and attack (82). Starting morale is 104, while hesitation is 16.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

The son of Ukita Naoie and one of the Five Elders under Toyotomi rule. He commanded one of the Western Army's largest forces at Sekigahara and fought Fukushima Masanori.

## Character and Reputation

He inherited immense responsibility at a young age and remained a major Toyotomi supporter. His long exile after Sekigahara became a defining part of his life.
