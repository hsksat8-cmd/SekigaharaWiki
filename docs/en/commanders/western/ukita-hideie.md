## In-Game Characteristics

This is a large unit, with notably high loyalty (86) and defense (82). Starting morale is 104, while hesitation is 16.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

Hideie inherited his house at a young age and rose through Hideyoshi's favor to become one of the Five Elders. He carried great status and responsibility while still young, led a major Western Army contingent at Sekigahara, and was later exiled following the defeat.

## Character and Reputation

Pride in his standing may have coexisted with anxiety about commanding a large force. Beyond proving his own courage, he had the burden of holding the Western Army's line and supporting its other commanders.

## The Person and Their Background

Hideie became a daimyo while young and attained high rank under Hideyoshi's patronage. The favors and expectations placed on him came with a need to prove the strength of the force entrusted to him. His closeness to the center of Toyotomi power was a source of pride, but it also raised the cost of a mistaken decision. Youthful ambition and the responsibility of leadership may well have existed side by side.

He fought as a leading Western Army commander at Sekigahara and was eventually sent into exile on distant Hachijojima. His life shows that even exalted rank offered little protection when the political order changed. The pride of fulfilling his duties was tested by a transformation beyond his control.
