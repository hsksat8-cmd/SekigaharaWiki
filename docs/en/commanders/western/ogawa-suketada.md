## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with notably high attack (56) and defense (55). Starting morale is 90, while hesitation is 76.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

A warrior from Omi who served under successive rulers, Suketada is among those said to have shifted from the Western to the Eastern side at Sekigahara. Few sources illuminate his private thoughts, though his position required him to weigh the fortunes of his army and household.

## Character and Reputation

His possible preference for adaptation over stubborn resistance reflects the difficult reality of commanders operating between more powerful lords.

## The Person and Their Background

Commanders such as Suketada did not determine national policy, yet the decisions of powerful men could decide the fate of their lands and retainers. Moving one's own unit within a large army was not solely a question of personal bravery. The actions of surrounding troops, obligations to one's lord, and uncertainty about treatment after the battle may all have influenced his judgment.

Traditions describe a change of allegiance at Sekigahara, but little evidence reveals what he thought. He may have felt torn between survival and warrior honor, though such possibilities must not be presented as established facts about his mind.
