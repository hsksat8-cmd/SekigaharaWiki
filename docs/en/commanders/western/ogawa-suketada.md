## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with particularly high attack (56) and defense (55). Starting morale is 90, while hesitation is 76.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

A daimyo under the Toyotomi government. He was positioned with the Western Army but changed sides during the battle and joined the attack on the Western right wing.

## Character and Reputation

Remembered mainly for changing sides at Sekigahara, though the loss of his lands afterward shows that the decision did not guarantee security.
