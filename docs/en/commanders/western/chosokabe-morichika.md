## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with notably high loyalty (74) and leadership (72). Starting morale is 97, while hesitation is 40.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

Morichika inherited the Chosokabe house, whose earlier rulers had once dominated much of Shikoku. After siding with the Western Army at Sekigahara, he lost his lands and later fought again in the Osaka campaigns.

## Character and Reputation

His determination to recover his family name and maintain ties to former retainers helps explain a life that returned to war after defeat. Pride in the house and refusal to accept its decline remained powerful themes.

## The Person and Their Background

Morichika succeeded to a house that had once commanded great power in Shikoku. Its history inspired pride, but the reality that he could not restore his father's former position likely placed heavy pressure on the young lord. Losing his domain after Sekigahara transformed not only his rank but also his relationship with those who had served the family.

His later return to war in the Osaka campaigns may be read as an attempt to recover a lost name and position. The stubborn determination to reshape his fate after defeat may have been rooted in his identity as heir to a proud house.
