## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with particularly high leadership (94) and attack (92). Starting morale is 109, while hesitation is 56.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

A veteran commander of the Shimazu clan of Satsuma. He joined the Western side with a small force and became famous for the Shimazu retreat through the enemy lines.

## Character and Reputation

A veteran known for composure, tactical experience, and the ability to preserve a small force under extreme pressure.
