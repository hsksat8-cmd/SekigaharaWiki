## In-Game Characteristics

This is a small unit, with notably high loyalty (96) and leadership (96). Starting morale is 108, while hesitation is 12.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

Yoshitsugu served Toyotomi Hideyoshi and is remembered through numerous stories about his friendship with Ishida Mitsunari, though not all can be verified from contemporary sources. He joined the Western Army at Sekigahara and died in the fighting.

## Character and Reputation

His decision to fulfill the role he had chosen despite the dangers suggests a warrior's concern for obligation and resolve. Later generations have found something compelling in a choice that cannot be explained by personal advantage alone.

## The Person and Their Background

Yoshitsugu served the Toyotomi regime in administrative and military affairs. Stories about his friendship with Mitsunari are famous, but some details were embellished by later generations. Even so, his decision to fight on the Western side at Sekigahara was real, and the weight of that choice cannot be measured by political calculation alone. He can be viewed as someone who combined awareness of danger with a determination not to abandon a responsibility he had accepted.

Holding one's position as danger approached could reflect a desire not to betray the trust of allies. Yet it is important not to reduce his actions to a simple story of friendship; the relationships formed through Toyotomi service and his own honor as a warrior also deserve consideration.
