## In-Game Characteristics

Leading 1,750 troops, Yoshikatsu stands out for his loyalty (94) and discipline (80). With attack and defense both at 76, his force is suited to holding formation on the front line. Starting morale is 80 and hesitation is 18.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

Remembered as a son of Otani Yoshitsugu, Yoshikatsu joined the Western Army alongside his father at the Battle of Sekigahara. Accounts place him in command of part of the Otani forces as they faced the enemy. Accounts differ on his life after Sekigahara.

## Character and Reputation

He is portrayed as a determined young commander conscious of his family’s honor and his duty to his father. Even when gaining ground, he tries to keep his ranks together; under pressure, he is ready to lead from the front and rally his men.

## The Person and Their Background

For Yoshikatsu, leading men under the Otani banner meant more than winning personal glory. He had to support his father Yoshitsugu’s command and uphold the family’s standing. An officer positioned near the front must be bold enough to seize an opening, yet careful enough not to let an advance dissolve into disorder. His emphasis on watching the enemy and maintaining formation can be read as the outlook of someone learning to carry responsibility for both a family and its soldiers.

When a battle turns against an army, a frontline commander must weigh the need to preserve his men against the need to shield those behind him. Yoshikatsu’s determination can therefore be understood not simply as youthful ambition, but as a willingness to hold a dangerous position for his father and his comrades. The surviving record cannot establish his private thoughts, but the duties of his position help explain the courage—and the pressure—that such a choice demanded.
