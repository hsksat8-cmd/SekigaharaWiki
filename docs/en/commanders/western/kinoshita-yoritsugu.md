## In-Game Characteristics

Leading 1,250 troops, Yoritsugu has high loyalty (94) and discipline (78). Defense and command confidence are both 76, making his force well suited to supporting adjacent units. Starting morale is 78 and hesitation is 22.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

Kinoshita Yoritsugu is remembered as a close relative of Otani Yoshitsugu and fought with the Western Army at Sekigahara. Accounts variously describe him as Yoshitsugu’s son, nephew, or adopted son, so the precise relationship remains uncertain. He is associated with the frontline Otani forces, although accounts of his later life also differ.

## Character and Reputation

He is portrayed as a composed officer who watches the wider battlefield, values coordination with neighboring units, and holds his assigned position. Under pressure, his strengths are reorganizing shaken troops, attending to the wounded, and refusing to give up prematurely.

## The Person and Their Background

As an officer within the Otani forces, Yoritsugu had to watch not only the enemy before him but also the movements of friendly formations on either side. When several units depend on one another, an overeager advance can be as dangerous as abandoning an assigned position: either may expose a neighbor’s flank. His caution is therefore best understood not as passivity, but as a sense of responsibility for the integrity of the entire line.

That responsibility becomes especially demanding when casualties mount and nearby formations begin to falter. Keeping men calm, restoring order, and looking for a path through a deteriorating battle can matter more than a dramatic charge. Although both his precise family relationship and his private motives remain uncertain, Yoritsugu’s position suggests a commander who placed the survival and cohesion of allied units above individual acclaim.
