## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with notably high leadership (80) and defense (76). Starting morale is 98, while hesitation is 48.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

As a powerful daimyo in Hitachi, Yoshinobu faced a difficult decision over his house's direction as the conflict between Ieyasu and Mitsunari deepened. His personal obligations and his political assessment may not always have pointed the same way.

## Character and Reputation

Although he was transferred to Dewa after the battle, his career can also be viewed through the independence of a lord trying to preserve his house amid a difficult political situation.

## The Person and Their Background

Yoshinobu had long navigated the complex alliances of northern Kanto as an influential lord of Hitachi. Even after gaining standing under the Toyotomi, the deepening split between Ieyasu and Mitsunari forced him to consider whether older relationships or the new political order deserved priority. Decisions were never simple when personal duty and the safety of one's household pulled in opposite directions.

Accounts differ on why he did not take decisive military action around Sekigahara. His later transfer to Dewa does not, by itself, establish indecision as a character flaw. It remains possible to see an independent spirit beneath his caution and a desire to remain true to his own obligations.
