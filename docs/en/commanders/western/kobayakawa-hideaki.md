## In-Game Characteristics

This is a large unit, with notably high attack (66) and defense (60). Starting morale is 86, while hesitation is 88.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

A young daimyo related to Toyotomi Hideyoshi, Hideaki was drawn into political struggles far beyond his personal control. His movement against the Western Army at Sekigahara became a decisive factor in the battle.

## Character and Reputation

Calculations about the future and pressure from other leaders may have shaped his decision, but his true feelings cannot be established with confidence. Seen as a young lord caught between honor, uncertainty, and survival, he is more complex than a simple label of traitor suggests.

## The Person and Their Background

Hideaki became a daimyo through his connections to the Toyotomi family and grew up amid appointments and political decisions he could not determine for himself. Advisers and powerful neighboring houses held considerable influence over a young lord. Different accounts explain his actions against the Western Army at Sekigahara, and the point at which his decision became final cannot be established simply.

Later portrayals often make him a symbol of betrayal. Yet his position may have combined a desire to maintain his honor, calculations about preserving his house, and anxiety at being caught between powerful rivals. A single battlefield decision should not be treated as a complete explanation of his character.
