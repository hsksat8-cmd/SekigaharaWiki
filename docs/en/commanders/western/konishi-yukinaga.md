## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with particularly high loyalty (72) and leadership (68). Starting morale is 96, while hesitation is 34.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

A Toyotomi commander and Christian daimyo who ruled southern Higo. He played major military and diplomatic roles during the Korean campaigns and fought for the Western Army at Sekigahara.

## Character and Reputation

A practical administrator, negotiator, and military commander. His Christian faith and diplomatic role distinguish him from many other daimyo of the period.
