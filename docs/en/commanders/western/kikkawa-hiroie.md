## In-Game Characteristics

This is a medium-sized unit, with notably high leadership (76) and defense (72). Starting morale is 95, while hesitation is 86.

!!! note
    These are fixed values used outside the random-ability scenario. In-game statistics are not intended as a direct historical judgment of the individual.

## Brief Career

As a member of the Mori clan, Hiroie placed great weight on the house's survival. At Sekigahara he negotiated with the Eastern side and helped restrain the Mori troops, disrupting Western Army plans while seeking to protect his family politically.

## Character and Reputation

His position brought short-term obligations into conflict with the long-term survival of the house. The tension between warrior honor and the interests of an entire clan is central to understanding his choice.

## The Person and Their Background

Hiroie's actions cannot be understood without his responsibilities as a member of the Mori house. By negotiating with the Eastern Army and restraining Mori forces at Sekigahara, he profoundly affected Western strategy. Those who emphasize loyalty to allies may condemn him, while others may interpret his action as a painful attempt to preserve his clan's territory and future.

Whatever judgment one adopts, the conflict between a warrior's honor and a family's survival remains important. Limiting losses in the event of defeat, rather than joining a bid for immediate victory, was a practical decision of a very different kind from celebrated battlefield heroics.
