# Mori Joins

[日本語版](../../scenarios/mori-joins.md)

The Mori forces begin by observing and then undergo checks to enter the battle. Their chance of joining is higher than normal, and the Western Army starts with +4 morale. Kobayakawa's defection is still possible.

In Version 2.8.0, standard-map battles can portray a what-if engagement **north of Mount Nangu** if **Mori Hidemoto's unit joins the fighting**. [Ankokuji Ekei](../commanders/western/ankokuji-ekei.md) has also been added as a Western Army commander.
