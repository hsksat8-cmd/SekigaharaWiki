# Eastern Advantage

[日本語版](../../scenarios/eastern-advantage.md)

The Eastern Army starts with +5 morale and the Western Army with -5. Kobayakawa's defection and the chain defections of Wakizaka, Kutsuki, Ogawa, and Akaza are more likely, while Mori and Shimazu are less likely to move. This scenario makes it easier for the Eastern Army to seize the initiative early.
