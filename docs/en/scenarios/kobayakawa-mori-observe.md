# Kobayakawa and Mori Observe

[日本語版](../../scenarios/kobayakawa-mori-observe.md)

In addition to Kobayakawa, Mori Hidemoto and Kikkawa Hiroie also tend to remain on the sidelines. Large-scale flank fighting is less likely, making this the scenario most focused on the central battle. It is currently the only scenario available in the trial version.
