# Mori and Kobayakawa Fight for the West

[日本語版](../../scenarios/mori-kobayakawa-western.md)

Both the Mori and Kobayakawa forces actively fight for the Western Army. The Western Army starts with +14 morale and the Eastern Army with -6. Most of the Western Army's major non-participation factors are removed, making this the fixed scenario most favorable to the Western Army.
