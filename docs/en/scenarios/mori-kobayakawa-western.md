# Mori and Kobayakawa Join the Western Army

[日本語版](../../scenarios/mori-kobayakawa-western.md)

This Western-favored what-if scenario has both the Mori and Kobayakawa forces fight for the Western Army.

The additional Western participation increases the danger of encirclement and defeat in detail for the Eastern Army. A successful central breakthrough led by Tokugawa Ieyasu may still reverse the numerical disadvantage.

!!! note
    The Microsoft Store description does not specify the exact initial objectives or entry times of the participating units.
