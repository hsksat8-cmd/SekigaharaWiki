# Version 2.7.0

[日本語版](../../updates/v2.7.0.md)

## Changes

- Added **Normal** and **All-Cavalry** as selectable unit compositions.
- For balance purposes, All-Cavalry units have their troop strength reduced by **15%**.
  - Example: converting a 5,000-troop unit to All-Cavalry reduces it to 4,250 troops.
- Increasing the troop strength of an All-Cavalry unit consumes **1.15 times** that amount from the army's troop-increase allowance.
  - Example: adding 1,000 cavalry consumes 1,150 troops from the allowance.
- All-Cavalry can only be selected for units with **6,600 troops or fewer**.
- Cavalry movement speed is now **1.5 times** that of Ashigaru.
- Unit scoring now normalizes troop exchange by the unit's troop strength at the start of battle.
- The title, menu, log panel, and status panel can now be collapsed.
- Updated troop strengths:
  - Maeda Toshinaga: **15,100**
  - Date Masamune: **14,100**
  - Niwa Nagashige: **3,700**
  - Yamauchi Kazutoyo: **3,000**
- In Fictional Grand Battle scenarios, **four medium-sized units** are randomly converted into All-Cavalry units to add more variation between battles.
- Made unit behavior more natural.

## Unit Score

Unit score is calculated as follows:

**Unit Score = (Enemy troops defeated − Own troops lost) ÷ Troops at the start of battle × 10,000 + Enemy commanders defeated × 3,000 + Enemy army commanders defeated × 10,000 − Own commanders defeated × 3,000 + Victory Bonus**

The Victory Bonus is **10,000 points** for a normal victory and **3,000 points** for a victory by troop superiority at 8 PM.

See [All-Cavalry Units](../systems/all-cavalry-units.md) for details of the new unit composition and [Results and Rankings](../gameplay/results-and-rankings.md) for scoring.
