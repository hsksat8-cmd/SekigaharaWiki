# Version 2.8.0

[日本語版](../../updates/v2.8.0.md)

## Commander Portraits and Event-Specific Dialogue

Portraits have been added for each commander, along with character-specific lines that appear during battle events. These additions make observing battles more immersive and help convey the commanders' individual personalities.

Related: [Observation and Real-Time Subtitles](../gameplay/observation.md) / [Commander List](../commanders/index.md)

## Ankokuji Ekei and Mori Hidemoto's What-If Participation

**Ankokuji Ekei** has been added as a Western Army commander. The standard-map scenarios now portray a hypothetical engagement **north of Mount Nangu** when **Mori Hidemoto's unit enters the battle**.

Related: [Ankokuji Ekei](../commanders/western/ankokuji-ekei.md) / [Mori Joins](../scenarios/mori-joins.md) / [Scenario List](../scenarios/index.md)

## Historically Adjusted Event Timing

The timing of **Tokugawa Ieyasu's advance** and **Kobayakawa Hideaki's defection** has been adjusted to occur closer to the historical sequence of events. This release note does not specify exact turns or guarantee a fixed in-game time for either event.

Related: [Historical Scenario](../scenarios/historical.md)

## Flank and Rear Engagement Restrictions

When the **combined troop strength** of enemy units engaging a unit **from its flank** reaches at least **one-fifth (20%)** of that unit's troop strength, the unit can no longer use **Charge, Aggressive, or Normal** tactics. By default, the unit switches to **Defensive** tactics when engaged from the flank and **Retreat** when engaged from the rear. These restrictions represent disruption to command and control when a unit is attacked from its flank or rear.

| Engagement direction | Angle relative to unit facing | Morale change per turn | Default tactic |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Rear | 140°–220° | −3.8 | Retreat |
| Flank | 40°–320°, excluding the rear sector | −3 | Defensive |

Other effects can also increase or decrease morale, so the total morale change in a turn may differ from the amounts shown above.

Related: [Flank and Rear Engagement](../systems/flank-rear-engagement.md) / [Morale](../systems/morale.md) / [Unit AI](../systems/ai.md)

## Fatigue Reduces Soldier-Dot HP

All **individual soldier dots** now gradually lose HP over time due to fatigue. No specific HP-loss rate or formula is provided in these notes.

Related: [Damage Calculation](../systems/damage.md)

## Adjustments to Starting Troop Counts

Some commanders' starting troop counts have been adjusted. See the [Commander List](../commanders/index.md) and [Fictional Grand Battle](../scenarios/fictional-grand-battle.md) for current baseline counts and standard/expanded map assignments. Random troop allocation and all-cavalry conversion may change actual starting strengths.
