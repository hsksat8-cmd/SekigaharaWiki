# Observation and Real-Time Subtitles

[日本語版](../../gameplay/observation.md)

Sekigahara: What-If Battle Simulator is designed to be enjoyable as an observation-focused game while you work.

## Autonomous Battle

After the battle begins, each unit acts autonomously according to its objectives and battlefield conditions. Constant input is not required.

## Automatic Overview Mode

In Automatic Overview Mode, the camera automatically cycles between the entire battlefield and the areas where combat is taking place, using animated battle views along the way. This allows you to understand both the overall situation and the fighting status of individual units simply by watching.

## Real-Time Subtitles

Real-time subtitles appear during the battle.

- They add dramatic context
- They make important developments easier to understand
- They help you follow the battle without constantly watching the entire map

## Switching Commander Details

While observing, you can select the commander whose details are displayed by using the mouse or the left and right arrow keys.

## Playing While Working

- Keep the battle visible in a corner of the screen
- Use subtitles to notice major changes
- Switch to a commander you want to follow
- Issue orders only when necessary
- Compare the same scenario under different deployments
