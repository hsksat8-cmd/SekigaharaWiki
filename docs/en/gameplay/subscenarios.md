# Sub-Scenarios

[日本語版](../../gameplay/subscenarios.md)

Customized conditions can be saved as sub-scenarios.

## Saved Information

Sub-scenarios support the following operations:

- Placement of created commanders
- Initial objectives
- Initial waiting times

## Editing Initial Deployment

Units can be dragged to freely change their initial positions. Deployment affects contact order, encirclement, central breakthroughs, and protection of the army commander.

## Import and Export

Groups of sub-scenarios can be imported and exported.

This allows customized conditions to be archived or moved together.

## Limits on User-Specified Troop Increases

- Existing commander units, except Tokugawa Ieyasu, are limited to 20,000 troops per unit.
- User commander units are also limited to 20,000 troops per unit.
- Tokugawa Ieyasu is exempt from the 20,000-per-unit cap. However, because manual additions to the Eastern Army are limited to +15,000 in total, his base force of 30,000 can be increased to at most 45,000 troops.
- Manually added troops are limited to a combined maximum of +15,000 above the base troop strength for each army.
- The entire troop count of a user commander consumes that army's +15,000 allowance.
- Increases to existing units use the same allowance. Reducing a unit does not restore the amount available for later increases.
- Values above the limits are adjusted automatically, with separate messages for the 20,000-per-unit limit and the +15,000-per-army limit.
