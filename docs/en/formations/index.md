# Formation Overview

[日本語版](../../formations/index.md)

Version 2.2.0 introduced five selectable formations.

| Formation | Description |
|---|---|
| Square Formation | A basic formation that preserves cohesion |
| Flexible Square Formation | A Square Formation with a maintenance duration of 0 turns; the default |
| Crane Wing Formation | Expands frontage and adapts well to uneven force sizes |
| Deep Formation | Strong in equal-size engagements but vulnerable when force sizes differ |
| Fish Scale Formation | Strong against Crane Wing and relatively adaptable to uneven force sizes |

## Formation Maintenance

- Flexible Square: 0 turns
- Square, Crane Wing, Deep, and Fish Scale: maintained for up to 50 turns or until contact with the enemy
- Default: Flexible Square

## Matchups

- Deep is generally strongest between units of equal size
- Crane Wing is generally weaker in equal-size battles
- Crane Wing can fight Deep on nearly equal terms
- Square has an advantage over Flexible Square
- Fish Scale has an advantage over Crane Wing
- When force sizes differ, Crane Wing performs best
- Fish Scale ranks second when force sizes differ
- Deep is the most fragile when force sizes differ
