# Formation Overview

[日本語版](../../formations/index.md)

According to the experiments, the formations have the following characteristics.

![Formation example from Version 2.2.0](../../assets/images/v2-2-0-formations-example.jpg){ .wiki-image }
<p class="image-caption">The five available formations</p>

## Square

A basic formation for frontal combat. Its depth and width are relatively even, making losses less likely to become concentrated in one area. It is suited to holding the main enemy force head-on and has the best overall performance.

It is versatile in almost every situation and is also superior to Flexible Square, the default formation. It should be the first choice when deploying the player's commander.

## Flexible Square

This formation does not maintain a fixed shape from the start, allowing its dots to respond more easily to targets and local battlefield conditions. It is suited to mobile support, including filling gaps between allied units, naturally concentrating after contact, and moving around an enemy flank.

## Crane Wing

Weak in frontal combat and intended for flank attacks when the enemy is already engaged head-on with another unit. If both wings reach around the enemy, flank and rear attack conditions become easier to create. It is not suited to a frontal attack against the main enemy force on its own.

In particular, it is highly unsuitable for use directly against the enemy front. It should be used when the enemy's flank or rear has been fixed in place.

## Deep

Its narrow frontage and greater depth make it ideal for entering narrow breakthrough points, concentrating on gaps in the enemy line, and breaking through the center of a Crane Wing formation. It is vulnerable to being flanked by broad-front formations such as Square and Fish Scale.

It is also effective when a small enemy force needs to be defeated quickly.

## Fish Scale

A wedge-shaped attacking formation that concentrates pressure on one point. It is suited to driving into a weakened front, flank, or rear. Alongside Square, it is the most consistently strong formation.

## Recommended Roles

Use Square for the main frontal line, Fish Scale for breakthroughs and concentrated attacks, Deep for exploiting gaps, Flexible Square for pursuit and support, and Crane Wing for flank attacks after the enemy has been fixed in place.
