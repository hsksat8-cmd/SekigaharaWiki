# Formation Overview

[日本語版](../../formations/index.md)

Units can use one of five formations: Square, Flexible Square, Crane Wing, Deep, and Fish Scale.

![Formation example from Version 2.2.0](../../assets/images/v2-2-0-formations-example.jpg){ .wiki-image }
<p class="image-caption">Example after the five formations were introduced</p>

## Formation Maintenance

- Flexible Square is a Square Formation with a maintenance duration of 0 turns
- Square, Crane Wing, Deep, and Fish Scale remain in formation for up to 50 turns or until contact with the enemy
- Flexible Square is the default

## Equal-Size Units

- Deep is stronger overall than the other formations
- Crane Wing is generally weaker than the others
- Crane Wing can nevertheless fight Deep on nearly equal terms
- Square has an advantage over Flexible Square
- Fish Scale has an advantage over Crane Wing

## Unequal-Size Units

- Crane Wing performs best whether facing a smaller or larger force
- Fish Scale ranks second
- Deep is the most fragile

!!! info "Sources"
    These relationships are described in the current Microsoft Store product description and the Version 2.2.0 development posts. Actual outcomes also depend on terrain, morale, fatigue, and interference from other units.
