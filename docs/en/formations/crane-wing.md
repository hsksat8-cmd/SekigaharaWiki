# Crane Wing Formation

[日本語版](../../formations/crane-wing.md)

Crane Wing spreads laterally to increase frontage.

## Characteristics

- Generally weaker in equal-size battles
- Can fight Deep on nearly equal terms
- Performs best when the two sides differ in size
- A smaller force may use controlled advance and retreat to wear down a larger enemy
- Disadvantaged against Fish Scale

## Difference from Historical Reality

The archived posts note that Crane Wing would normally be unfavorable for a smaller force in real combat. In the game, however, small units can behave with unusually high coordination and mobility, allowing them to exploit a wide frontage.
