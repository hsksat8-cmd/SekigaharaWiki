<div class="hero" markdown="1">

This is the official wiki for **Sekigahara IF Battle Formation Simulator**, a game in which you change formations, troop strength, morale, and commander behavior, then observe how the battle develops.

## Main Features

<div class="stat-grid">
  <div class="stat-card"><strong>Autonomous units</strong><br>Units act according to morale, position, battlefield conditions, and other factors.</div>
  <div class="stat-card"><strong>What-if scenarios</strong><br>Test alternate outcomes such as Kobayakawa remaining neutral or joining the Western Army.</div>
  <div class="stat-card"><strong>Five formations</strong><br>Square, Flexible Square, Crane Wing, Deep, and Fish Scale.</div>
  <div class="stat-card"><strong>Observe or command</strong><br>Watch the battle or command the Eastern Army, Western Army, or player-created commanders.</div>
</div>
