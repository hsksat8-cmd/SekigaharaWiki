# Morale

[日本語版](../../systems/morale.md)

Morale is one of the most important factors in battle.

## Conditions That Raise Morale

- Gaining the upper hand
- Approaching victory
- Driving the enemy back

## Conditions That Lower Morale

- Repeated setbacks
- Accumulated fatigue
- A worsening battle situation
- Encirclement
- Direct collision with a much stronger force

When morale falls, troop losses can accelerate rapidly.

## Retreat Behavior in Version 2.2.0

Version 2.2.0 added temporary retreat behavior when morale drops. This helps prevent small units from instantly collapsing at zero morale and allows them to alternate between withdrawal and renewed attacks.
