# Unit Types

[日本語版](../../systems/unit-types.md)

Soldier dots are divided into the following unit types.

| Unit type | Features / Strengths | Weaknesses / Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Spear Ashigaru | The basic troop type. Uses standard HP and handles close combat. If an enemy Matchlock Ashigaru is within distance 4, it charges the nearest enemy Matchlock Ashigaru for 10 turns regardless of morale. It can also be assigned to guard Matchlock Ashigaru. | Has no ranged attack and may take fire while closing into the Matchlock Ashigaru's firing range. At low morale, if enemy Matchlock Ashigaru are nearby, it retreats toward a safer allied area. |
| Matchlock Ashigaru | Range 1–4. Accuracy and damage increase at shorter distances. Fires when no ally is in the line of fire and the target is not engaged in close combat with an ally. Can fire once every 4 turns. | HP is 70% of a Spear Ashigaru's. Cannot fire at targets at a distance below 1 and retreats when enemies get too close. To prevent friendly fire, it does not shoot enemies engaged with allied troops. Without guards, it is vulnerable to charges by Spear Ashigaru and cavalry. |
| Cavalry | Has 1.5 times the HP of a normal Spear Ashigaru. Can be used to charge enemy Matchlock Ashigaru and can also be assigned to guard friendly Matchlock Ashigaru. While guarding, cavalry stays near friendly Matchlock Ashigaru and intercepts enemies that come within distance 1. | At present, cavalry has no dedicated bonus over Spear Ashigaru to the normal movement limit or melee attack power itself. About 10% of ordinary troops are cavalry in the initial deployment. |
| Commander Dot | A high-durability command center located near the center of the unit. It has more HP than ordinary troops, and the unit banner is always displayed. | It is neither Matchlock Ashigaru nor cavalry and is excluded from the ordinary-troop logic for anti-matchlock charges and matchlock guarding. When isolated or in danger, it prioritizes survival. |
| Army Commander Dot | An even more durable unit center than a Commander Dot. Defeating the army commander has a major impact on the battle, and its banner is displayed larger than those of ordinary troops. | Like a Commander Dot, it is handled separately from ordinary troop-type logic. When isolated, retreat and guard-priority behavior is applied. |
