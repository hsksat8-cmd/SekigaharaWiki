# Unit Size and Defeat in Detail

[日本語版](../../systems/unit-size.md)

## Total Troop Count Does Not Directly Decide Dot-to-Dot Combat

According to the archived posts, the total size of a unit does not directly determine the strength of each soldier-dot engagement. Unit attack, defense, morale, and related values matter more.

## Strength of Large Units

Large units become especially dangerous when they defeat isolated smaller units one by one. In the 30,000-strong Tokugawa force versus the Western Army's main force, the Tokugawa unit gradually destroyed separated Western units.

## Strength of Small Units

After Version 2.2.0 introduced temporary retreat behavior, small units became better able to move in and out of combat and resist a single large unit through coordinated maneuver.
