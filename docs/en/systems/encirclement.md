# Encirclement and Central Breakthrough

[日本語版](../../systems/encirclement.md)

## Soldier-Dot Encirclement

At the individual soldier-dot level, encirclement is clearly advantageous.

## Unit-Level Encirclement

At the unit level, encirclement has both benefits and risks.

### Benefits

- Lowers enemy morale
- Increases contact frontage
- Restricts retreat routes

### Risks

- Spreads out your own troops
- Weakens the center
- May allow the enemy to break through and decide the battle

The Western Army may be forced to rely on encirclement because its commander unit is weaker, but it must remain alert to a central breakthrough by a large force such as Tokugawa Ieyasu's unit.
