# Deep Formation vs. Square Formation

[日本語版](../../experiments/deep-vs-square.md)

Two equal-size units with identical parameters fought twice, with the formations swapped.

## Experiment 1

- Eastern Army: Deep Formation
- Western Army: Square Formation
- All other conditions: identical

## Experiment 2

- Eastern Army: Square Formation
- Western Army: Deep Formation
- All other conditions: identical

## Observed Pattern

In both battles, the Deep Formation applied strong pressure through the center and tended to split the Square Formation. Once the enemy was divided, the Deep Formation defeated the separated sections in detail.

Swapping the formations also reversed which army held the advantage, suggesting that formation matchup had a major effect under these controlled conditions.

!!! caution
    This result applies to a specific in-game setup. Terrain, troop imbalance, multiple-unit interference, fatigue, and encirclement may change the outcome.
