# Comprehensive Pincer Attack Experiments

[日本語版](../../../experiments/pincer/index.md)

[Experiments](../index.md)

This section brings together experiments on **pincer attacks**, in which a frontal unit and a rear unit attack the same enemy from opposite directions while formations, matchlock ratios, unit tactics, and other conditions are varied.

In a pincer attack, the frontal unit is generally responsible for engaging and holding the enemy, while the rear unit applies sustained pressure from behind. As a result, the same formation or matchlock ratio can perform differently depending on whether it is used at the front or the rear.

## Experiments

- [Pincer Attack Experiment](../../formations/pincer-experiment.md)  
  A broad comparison of formations, matchlock ratios, and force differences with a 5,000-troop Western frontal unit and a 5,000-troop Western rear unit attacking the Eastern Army from both sides.
- [Rear Unit: Deep Formation and Matchlock Ratio Comparison](rear-deep-matchlock.md)  
  With the Eastern Army at 12,200 troops, the Western frontal unit fixed at Deep, 30% matchlocks, and Normal, and the rear unit fixed at Deep and Charge, this experiment compares 30% and 45% matchlock ratios for the rear unit.
